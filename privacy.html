What we collect

  • Your phone number, used to sign you in and to find friends who also use Chat Up.
  • Your name, photo and about text, if you add them.
  • Your messages, photos and voice notes. These are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. We cannot read them.
  • Your text and photo statuses (encrypted) and video statuses (not encrypted).
  • Call details: who called whom, when, and for how long. Call audio and video are encrypted between phones.
  • Technical data: your push notification token, device type and app version.

What we cannot see

Message text, photos, voice notes, text and photo statuses and call media are locked with keys that stay on your phone. We keep an encrypted copy of your key backup only if you create one, and we cannot open it. If you forget your backup passphrase, nobody can recover your old messages.

What we can see

Your phone number, profile, who you chat or call with and when, group membership, video statuses, and anything you choose to send us in a report.

How we use it

To run the app, deliver messages and calls, send notifications, keep people safe, and handle reports. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who helps us run Chat Up

Supabase (database and storage), Twilio (sign-in text messages), LiveKit (calls), and Apple and Google (push notifications). They only process data to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Statuses are deleted after 24 hours. Messages stay until you or the other person delete them for everyone. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, messages you sent, statuses and key backup. [LAWYER: confirm what must be kept by law, such as reports, and for how long]
